Before you reply.

What exactly is an MVP?

A minimum viable product: the smallest version real users can try. You learn what to build next before you spend more.

Who owns the code and the accounts?

You do. The code lives in your repository and the hosting, database and store accounts are in your name from day one. The app works without me.

What do you build with?

Usually Next.js for the web, Expo for iPhone and Android, Supabase for accounts and data, Vercel for hosting. If your team already uses something else, tell me in the brief.

What happens after launch?

If something I built doesn't work as agreed, I fix it at no charge. New features are quoted the same way as the first version.

Why one person and not an agency?

You talk to the person who builds it, so nothing gets lost between a salesperson and a developer. I take a few projects at a time, and I'll give you the start date before you commit.

What should your software do on day one?

Describe the job in plain words. I'll suggest the smallest version worth building and what it would cost.
