01 · Support operationsn8n + Gemini · Nov 2026

AI Support Triage.

Testing, failure analysis and human review

A workflow that checks each incoming support request, sorts it by topic and drafts a reply from approved help articles. A person reviews every draft before anything is sent. Practice project, tested on 24 made-up requests.

Sorted right

Review time
100→45s
  • Bad input Two repeats recorded without overwriting the first draft. Two requests with missing data rejected, with the reason saved.
  • Bug found Test #48 reported success, but 22 saved drafts had lost their request number. I traced it, fixed how the number passes between steps and added a check before saving.
  • Retest In test #50 I matched every saved draft to its request and confirmed earlier records were intact.
  • Honest Review time is from five requests only. Editing time and running costs are not measured yet, and the page says so.

03 · IntegrationsMake · own-product MVP

Fitness plan automation

A Make scenario that turned a questionnaire into a personalised PDF plan and handed the link to the email tool: Tally → OpenAI → CloudConvert → Dropbox → MailerLite. I wrote the rules and prompt, designed the PDF and debugged the scenario.

  • Evidence I kept: a blueprint with 12 modules and 29 mapped fields, 14 archived PDFs, the email automation settings.
  • Limits I found: the export was checked only once, two PDFs had layout errors, and 50% of the eight emails bounced. The MVP had no buyers.

04How I work

Woven from the best. Built to ship.

Every ingredient exists; the combination doesn't. That's how I designed CreatorZen. I studied the best creator tools and habit apps, took the one mechanism each got right, and wove them around the question none of them asks: how much can you actually create today?

✳The receipt

Compiled from my CreatorZen research notes — starting with “Competitive landscape & feature gaps” — and checked line by line against the code that actually shipped.

SourceWhat it provesWhat I took
DuolingoA character and a streak turn a tool into a daily habit.Zenny — the camera mascot who picks today's idea — plus check-in streaks.
vidIQAn idea needs a reason to believe it, not just a title.“Why this idea”, built only from real signals. I skipped their numeric score on purpose — a made-up number is a hallucination with a progress bar.
LaterContent pillars turn random posts into a strategy.Your pillars are set in onboarding, and new ideas are generated inside them.
TypefullyAI writing only works when it sounds like you.Profile import: Apify + an LLM read your public profile, so day-one ideas already fit your niche and tone.
Predis.aiThe fewer steps from “nice idea” to “ready post”, the better.One-tap plans: hook, script, caption and images on one screen.
CaptionsThe script and the camera belong in one flow.Scripts laid out line by line, teleprompter-style, inside the shooting plan.
My threadNo creator tool asks how much you can do today.A 10-second check-in → Energy Score from 0 to 100 → today's load: 1, 2 or 4 tasks.

Refused to copyA full scheduler, a social inbox, a video editor, an AI avatar studio. Crowded, expensive — and they would have turned CreatorZen into one more tool instead of a calm second pilot.
