02Method

Every ingredient exists. The combination doesn't.

That's how I built CreatorZen. I studied the best creator tools and habit apps, took the one mechanism each got right, and wove them around the question none of them asks: how much can you actually create today?

  1. 01

    Study the best

    I map the strongest products around the problem — creator tools, habit apps, AI writers — and write down what each one gets exactly right.

  2. 02

    Pull one thread from each

    One proven mechanic per product, with a note on why it works — and what has to change so it fits my users.

  3. 03

    Add the thread nobody had

    No creator tool asked how tired you are. So CreatorZen starts with a 10-second energy check-in that decides today's workload.

  4. 04

    Weave, ship, measure

    Everything in one loop — check in, pick, film, post. Then real users, PostHog funnels and an honest call on what to fix next.

05How I think

How I think

I don't wait for perfect conditions. No team, no funding, no permission — and I still shipped. When I hit a wall, I study how the best got past it, pull the thread, and find a way. My ambitions are bigger than my budget, and I know nobody builds anything great alone. So I'm looking for a team with harder problems than I can solve on my own — a place to contribute from day one, and to grow.

— Dmytro

I always find a way.

Responsibility isn't a trait, it's a habit. I own the outcome, not just my task — and if the road is closed, I build a bridge.

Study the best, then combine.

Originality is a new combination of proven threads. I write down what I take — and why it works.

If I can't draw it, I don't get it yet.

I visualise flows, systems and data before I build them. Diagrams are how I think.

Pre-mortem first.

Imagine it already failed. List why. Design those failures out — then commit.

Ship, measure, decide.

Stopping a product is a result, not a failure. Data gets the final vote.

Calm is a skill.

Learned at 11,000 metres with a full cabin watching. Pressure makes me clearer, not louder.
