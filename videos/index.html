Five ads, and how they were made.

CreatorZen: from "no ideas" to a ready post

Own app · 24 s · 9:16 · TikTok, Reels, Shorts

CreatorZen is my iOS app for content creators. The ad follows a tired creator who has nothing to post. An AI presenter says the hook full screen, then shrinks into a corner while real screen recordings play: the energy check-in, picking an idea, the content plan and a 7-slide carousel the app generates.

My part
Script, AI presenter, editing, captions, motion graphics, music, frame-by-frame QA in TikTok and Reels safe zones
Note
The end card says the presenter is AI-generated. This is my product, so there are no campaign results to quote.

Bodyclimb: from zero pull-ups to the first one

Own app · 15 s · 9:16 · two opening hooks

Bodyclimb is a training app I'm building. The app asks how many pull-ups you can do, and "None yet" gets tapped. A step-by-step plan and a climb counter fill up, and the first pull-up lands on the beat as the counter turns to 1.

Same ad, two openings to test against each other: "Can't do a single pull-up?" and "0 pull-ups. Still climbing."

My part
Concept, storyboard, app UI animation, motion graphics, AI-generated athlete clips (labelled in the ad), sound, QA

VELTO: one orange line through a morning

Spec ad · 18 s + 6 s cutdown · 9:16

A spec ad for a premium travel mug, made from a creative brief. The brand isn't real. The mug's thin orange ring becomes a line through one morning: home, a train over the bay, a café. The camera stays level with the ring, so every horizon runs through it.

The places are AI-generated. The mug isn't: it's my own 3D model, lit and composited into each shot, so its shape, colour and logo never drift.

My part
Concept, 3D product model, AI scenes, compositing, editing, music, logo checks on every frame

Rift Rovers: one project, nine finished videos

Spec ad · 30 s · 3 hooks × 3 formats = 9 files

A mobile game ad in the "pro creator tips" style: a top-3 weapons countdown for a legendary hover tank, gameplay with kill pop-ups, a promo code screen and an end card. Rift Rovers isn't a real game. I made it up.

Three hooks (free legendary, wrong loadout, one tank with three weapons) in 16:9, 1:1 and 9:16. Each format is laid out on its own inside TikTok, Reels and Shorts safe zones, not cropped from another. Every visual is AI-generated, and the tank looks the same in every shot.

My part
Script, AI visuals, game UI, editing, sound, all nine layouts

Northbar Futures: a trading platform, shown from the inside

Spec ad · 68 s · 16:9 · YouTube

A spec commercial for a futures prop trading platform. I invented Northbar Futures, its terminal and its Polaris AI assistant. Cinematic AI shots open it, then you're inside the terminal, animated like a screen recording: live P&L, a drawdown buffer, risk rules and the assistant explaining a trade stopped out by a CPI spike. It ends on a payout.

My part
Script, AI visuals, UI design and animation, voiceover, music edit, 80 sound cues
Note
Wording kept safe for a regulated niche: simulated capital, illustrative numbers, a risk disclaimer.
