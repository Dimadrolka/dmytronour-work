Personalized Fitness Plan Fulfillment

A Make workflow that turned a training questionnaire into a personalized PDF and prepared the link for email delivery.

Historical own-product MVP · Make + Tally + OpenAI

See what I verified

From questionnaire to a readable PDF.

The idea was to offer a training plan tailored to a person’s answers. I wrote the training rules and prompt, designed the PDF layout, and assembled and debugged the Make scenario by hand. I used AI chat for setup and troubleshooting help.

  1. 01

    Collect answers

    Tally collects the intake. Make maps the answers into a structured request.

  2. 02

    Generate HTML

    OpenAI uses my rules and template to generate the plan.

  3. 03

    Render and store

    CloudConvert renders the PDF. Make downloads a copy and stores it in Dropbox.

  4. 04

    Prepare delivery

    Make passes the name and PDF URL to MailerLite. A separate group-triggered automation uses them in the email.

What the archive could prove.

The saved blueprint contains 12 modules and 29 mapped fields. The archive contains 14 PDFs. The MailerLite configuration shows the group trigger and personalization fields. Its activity snapshot shows eight emails sent and a 50% bounce rate.

The MVP had zero buyers. The evidence comes from saved configurations, archived outputs and an activity snapshot. A fresh end-to-end run and individual delivery would still need verification.

What I would check before relaunching

The scenario checked for a finished export once. I’d test delayed conversion and add bounded polling before download. I’d also verify which link reaches the inbox and how long it stays valid. The archive review found a formatting artifact in one file and a footer overlap in another, so I’d add a PDF layout check before delivery.
