05Questions

Ask me anything.

Why is it this affordable? What's the catch?

There's no catch. I work alone, with no office and no account managers, and AI tools handle the repetitive parts. You pay for the work, not for an agency around it.

Are there any other costs?

No. The domain is registered in your name and I pay for the first two years. Hosting has no monthly fee. Care is optional.

What if I don't like it?

You see the demo before you decide, you get two rounds of changes, and you pay only once the site is live. If you say no, you owe nothing.

Who owns the site?

You do. The domain is in your name and you get every password. The site works without me.

Do I have to take Care?

No. Without it, the site stays online. You pay only for the domain renewal after two years, at the registrar's price, and for any change you ask for, at a price we agree up front.

Will I be first on Google?

Nobody can honestly guarantee first place. I do everything that counts: a fast site, text written for “service + your city” searches, and a link to your Google Business Profile. With good reviews, your chances are good.

What exactly is the free demo?

A preview built from your public information, so you can see the idea. The finished site, with confirmed details, your photos and your own domain, comes after you say yes.

How long does it take?

5–7 days from the moment I have your details, 10–14 for rentals. If the details come later, the date moves with them.

What counts as a round of changes?

You collect your corrections in one message and I apply them together. You get two of those. New pages or new features are separate work, at a price we agree first.

Where is it hosted, and who has the files?

On a hosting service with no monthly fee for a site like this. Along with the passwords you get all the site’s files, so you can move it wherever you like.

How do I pay?

Once the site is live, I send you a secure card payment link. Your card details never reach me.

What happens after launch?

If something I delivered doesn’t work, I fix it at no charge. For regular changes there’s Care, or you pay per change.

What do you need from me?

Twenty minutes: services, prices, opening hours, a phone number for bookings, and your permission to use the photos and reviews.

Want to see your own site?

Send me your business. I'll build a free preview and send you the link.
